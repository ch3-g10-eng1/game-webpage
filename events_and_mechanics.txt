 Game Events and Mechanics

  Hidden Events

  These are invisible triggers placed randomly in the maze that activate when the player walks over them.

  Darkness Event
  - Triggered once when player steps on a hidden tile
  - Reduces visibility for 10 seconds by applying a dark overlay (65% opacity)
  - Player can still see the maze layout but with significantly reduced clarity
  - Triples final score if completed while active

  Lecturer Event
  - Spawns a second enemy lecturer when triggered
  - Adds an additional threat to navigate around
  - Also provides a 3x score multiplier if active at game completion

  Dynamic Event

  Super Enemy
  - Spawns automatically when player reaches the center of the maze (within 5 units of the midpoint)
  - Moves faster than regular lecturers (2.5x speed)
  - Only appears once per game
  - Can damage player on contact unless shield is active

  Collectible Powerups

  Keys (3 per maze)
  - Required to unlock the exit gates
  - Must collect all keys before finishing the level

  Book
  - Provides a one-time shield that blocks damage from enemies or fire
  - Shield is consumed when hit

  Coffee
  - Doubles player movement speed permanently
  - One-time pickup

  Coins (10 per maze)
  - Each coin adds 1500 points
  - Collecting all coins multiplies total score by 8x

  Hazards

  Fire Laptops (2 per maze)
  - Removes one heart on contact unless shield is active
  - Disappears after being triggered

  Water Spills (2 per maze)
  - Reduces player speed by 20%
  - Effect is permanent and stacks with multiple hits
